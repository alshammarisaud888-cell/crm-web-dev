# Saudi Sensing CRM V3.9

## Lead Workspace

The blue Lead ID opens a dedicated full-page Lead Workspace containing:

- Lead ID
- Account ID and Account
- Company
- Contact and Job Title
- Email and Mobile
- Lead Source and Interest Area
- Lead Status and Score
- Estimated Value
- Owner
- Next Action Date
- Notes
- Linked Account details

## Convert Lead to Opportunity

A Convert button is available in:

- The Leads table
- Lead Workspace

The conversion window:

- Generates the next sequential Opportunity ID automatically
- Uses the same `OPP-xxxxx` sequence
- Keeps the linked Account
- Carries over estimated value, interest area, owner and notes
- Allows selection of stage, probability and expected close date
- Changes the Lead status to `Converted`
- Opens the newly created Opportunity Workspace

Converted Leads cannot be converted a second time from the normal button.
